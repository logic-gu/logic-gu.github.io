I am a mathematical logician whose research work is focused on model theory of arithmetic and set theory, and axiomatic theories of truth. 
I worked at GU as Professor of Logic from May 2013 to July 2020. Since my retirement, I have been affiliated with GU as Emeritus Professor of Logic.
