# Abstract
This thesis concerns semantic set-lifting procedures in logic, particularly plurivalence and team semantics. These are methods of constructing new logical semantics in terms of collections of semantic objects from an initial logical semantic (for the studied cases in terms of sets of truth values or teams of assignments respectively). By representing the underlying semantics in terms of algebraic structures we present versions of these set-lifting constructions in terms of _power algebras_ [Brink, 1992], that is, algebras of subsets with pointwise operations in an underlying algebraic structure. Doing so, and by considering the classes of such algebras, we present versions of the set-lifting procedures that are independent of the semantic representation of the original logic.

This thesis includes three papers presenting applications of the power-algebra interpretation of set-lifting procedures in three different cases. Paper I presents two new syntactic categorisations of a family of eight logics called the FDE-family, previously categorised semantically by Priest. These categorisations are also extended to larger families of logics. Paper II describes Priest's notion of plurivalence in terms of power matrices and shows that reiterating the plurivalent construction is logically equivalent to a single application. Paper III (joint work with Fredrik Engström) instead deals with team semantics, and presents the logic of team properties (LTP) as a natural algebraically defined abstract team-semantic extension of classical propositional logic. LTP is a team logic closed under substitution (a property that is missing for most logics with team semantics) and still expressive enough to encode many traditional team logics as axiomatisable syntactic fragments.
